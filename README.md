### About

Robotics engineer based in **Kotte, Sri Lanka**.

I build systems that have to survive contact with the real world — so I work in
simulation first, log everything, and keep the results reproducible. Most of my time
goes to motion, balance and control, with a parallel track in deep learning.

Open to collaboration and freelance work.
